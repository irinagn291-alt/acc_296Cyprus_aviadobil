Cordillera is a private reading tracker. The book you are on is drawn as a ridge; you pin the pages you read today and the profile climbs toward the last page, which the app calls the summit. It is for one reader who wants an on-device expedition through a volume, not a social shelf or a storefront.

No credentials or demo account are needed. A cold launch shows the system launch screen (no titles or buttons), then the introduction: three pages, each with Skip and Continue. Skip on any page, or Continue on page 3, ends the introduction. Page 1 of 3: The book becomes a ridge. Cordillera draws the volume you are reading as an elevation profile. Page 2 of 3: Pin the pages you read. Each pin moves you along the ridge toward the summit of the book. Page 3 of 3: Close one gap this week. A day without a pin leaves a dashed gap. Bridge closes the oldest one. First launch then shows No expedition yet / Open a volume. Tap Open to open Pack.
